WELCOME TO MY PAGE

I’m Lucas. I’m an IT Engineer based currently in Kraków, but this page isn't really about my CV, it’s about the stuff that keeps me busy when the terminal is closed (and sometimes when it’s open).

The Work Side of Things

I’ve spent the last few years living inside Linux and tinkering with it, fixing problems from a distance and improving them. I’m currently and constantly working with things like Azure, Jamf, and Bash. I like the fast-paced nature of it, but mostly I just like fixing things. If something is broken, I want to be the one to find out why.

The Machine

I’m a big believer in knowing exactly what’s happening under the hood. After years of distro-hopping and breaking my OS just to see if I could fix it, I’ve settled on Debian running on a ThinkPad. It’s predictable, it’s clean, and most importantly, it has zero bloat. I treat my digital workspace the same way I treat my life: if it doesn't serve a purpose, it doesn't need to be there.

Beyond the Screen

When I’m not wrangling endpoints or writing scripts, I’m usually looking for a bit of nostalgia or a bit of grease.

Retro Gaming: I have a massive appreciation for emulators. There’s something about being able to fire up a game from twenty years ago on modern hardware that never gets old. It’s digital preservation, but mostly it’s just fun.

Engines & History: I’ve always been drawn to cars and motorcycles. It’s not just about the speed; it’s about the history and the stories behind the machines. Every bike or car has a narrative of how it was built and where it’s been. There’s a mechanical honesty in an engine that you don't always find in software.

I’m always around, usually sharpening some skill or another or planning my next road trip.

Thanks for stopping by my corner of the web :)
